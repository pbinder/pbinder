### 👋 Hello! I'm Paul Binder — Co-founder & Technical Lead at Enquant.

I'm building **Enquant**, an operational decision intelligence platform for supply chains. We model a company's real-world constraints and objectives, then deploy mathematical solvers and AI agents to turn thousands of everyday operational decisions — routing, scheduling, allocation, fulfilment — into optimal ones. Our first proof-of-concept delivered a **16% cost reduction**.

Before founding Enquant, I spent years building robust ESG solutions and energy-management systems and modernizing enterprise-scale applications, leading diverse, cross-functional remote teams.

My expertise lies in:
* **Product & Full-Stack Engineering:** React, TypeScript, Next.js, Monorepo, Node.js, REST APIs, MUI, Redux, Responsive Design, Angular.
* **Decision Intelligence & Optimization:** Mathematical solvers, AI agents, data modelling, Python.
* **Technical Leadership:** Building products from zero, leading distributed teams, driving architecture refactoring, and establishing CI/CD pipelines.
* **Cloud & Backend:** Azure Cloud, C#, SQL, Docker, Vercel.

I'm dedicated to turning complex problems into seamless products, optimizing performance, and building teams that ship.

Feel free to explore my pinned repositories to see some of my work!
